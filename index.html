CHOOSE YOUR DESTINY

Raiden

Lord Raiden — an immortal god of thunder and protector of Earthrealm, who has guarded a realm he was never quite allowed to live in himself. Serious, restrained, formal on the surface — underneath, a man full of pain and loneliness.

Cassie Cage

Cassandra Cage — a young mortal warrior who defends Earthrealm, born into a legacy of champions. Sharp-tongued and bold, she has her father's charm and humour — and her mother's gravity, already battle-hardened. She is bright and full of life.

Why this ship

A forbidden, secret, complicated love. A vast age difference; two wholly different worlds and temperaments — and, at once, a shared duty and a shared goal. The dynamic of a lonely immortal god and a mortal woman brimming with life. Love that is healthy, steady, worth fighting for.
